Frequently Asked Questions

What do I get for the $99 setup fee?
Your $99 covers the full design and build of your listing page — including your choice of layout, adding your logo, photos, business details, and publishing it to the FindLocal directory. You'll be live within 48 hours.
Is the $20/year mandatory?
Yes, the annual $20 keeps your listing hosted and active in the directory. If you choose not to renew, your listing will be removed. There are no hidden fees beyond these two charges.
Can I update my listing later?
Absolutely. Minor content updates (hours, phone number, description) are included in your annual fee. Major redesigns may incur a small additional charge.
Do I need to provide my own photos?
Photos are optional but highly recommended. If you don't have any, we can use free stock images that match your business type.
